Bogotá's urban trees

Year 2026
Role Data processing, design and code
Stack PMTiles, MapLibre, Next.js
Visit See the map

A map of the 1,473,588 trees in Bogotá's public tree census, 423 species, from the open data of the Jardín Botánico de Bogotá.

I converted the official shapefile into vector tiles packed in a single PMTiles file, so the whole city loads from static hosting, with no tile server, over HTTP range requests.

A statistics panel summarizes species and heights (the most common tree is the sauco, and the average height is 4.2 m), and a mini-map keeps you oriented in the city.
