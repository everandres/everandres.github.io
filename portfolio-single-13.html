Mielenliikutus

Year 2026
Role Data, design and code
Stack DuckDB, GeoParquet, H3, Astro, MapLibre
Visit Site

Around the world, about 65,000 places carry a feeling in their name: El Porvenir, Loma El Olvido, Cerro del Diablo, Quebrada La Peligrosa. Mielenliikutus, a Finnish word for being moved, is an atlas of those names. I read them from GeoNames in 29 languages and sorted them into five emotions: hope, melancholy, fear, love and peace.

The data side runs on DuckDB and GeoParquet: a dictionary of emotional words per language, rules to drop false friends such as surnames and literal descriptions, H3 hexagons for the world view and PMTiles so the whole atlas is a static site.

The entry point is a circle of more than a hundred countries, joined when they named their land with the same feelings in similar proportions. Choosing a country turns the circle into a tree of what it shares with the others, and every root opens the real places behind it. From there you can travel to the globe and down to each name on the map.

Names are slippery, so the atlas says where it can be wrong: each country carries a note on the doubts that come with its language.
