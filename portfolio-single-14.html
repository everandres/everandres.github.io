Outliers

Year 2026
Role Data, design and code
Stack DuckDB, Parquet, Wikidata, TypeScript, SVG
Visit Site

In Outliers, Malcolm Gladwell noticed that elite young hockey players are mostly born early in the year. Children compete by birth year, so whoever is born just after the cutoff is the oldest, and often the strongest, in their age group: they get picked more, train more and go further. I wanted to see whether that holds for everyone famous enough to have a Wikipedia page.

The data joins the cross-verified database of notable people (Laouenan et al., 2022) with exact birth dates from Wikidata: 1.7 million people from every era. DuckDB and Parquet do the counting. Each month is compared with what its number of days would predict, and a χ² test decides whether a pattern is real or chance.

Across all famous people the tilt is small: 5% more births between January and March. Among athletes it is twice as strong, and it follows each country’s calendar. The United Kingdom peaks in September, when its school year starts.

The clock is the interface. Bars leave or enter the circle of the expected, the hand points to the months that stand out, and tapping a month lists the five most visible people born in it. Those known for crimes are not hidden; they get a list of their own.
